HOW TO PLAY — 21 WITH DICE
===========================

OBJECTIVE
---------
Roll dice to get as close to 21 as possible without going over.
The dealer (computer) is your opponent. Beat the dealer's total to win!

GAME FLOW
---------
1. The dealer rolls first and reveals only one of its dice to you.
   The rest remain hidden.

2. It's your turn. Choose to roll 1, 2, or 3 dice.
   The values are added to your running total.

3. You may roll up to 4 times per hand.
   After each roll, you can choose to STAND (keep your total)
   or roll again.

4. If your total goes over 21, you BUST and the dealer wins.

5. After you stand (or use all 4 rolls), the dealer reveals
   its hidden dice and completes its turn.

6. The dealer must roll again if its total is 16 or less.
   The dealer must stand when its total reaches 17 or higher.

7. The highest total at or below 21 wins.
   Ties go to the dealer.

INSTANT WIN
-----------
If you roll three sixes (18) on your FIRST roll, you win
instantly — the dealer doesn't get to finish!

STRATEGY TIPS
-------------
- Watch the dealer's revealed die. A high die may mean the
  dealer already has a strong hand. A low die may be a bluff.

- Rolling 1 die is safe but slow. Rolling 3 dice is fast but
  risky. Mix it up based on your total.

- You don't have to use all 4 rolls. Sometimes standing at
  17 or 18 is the smart play.

- The dealer MUST hit on 16 or below, which means the dealer
  can bust too. A conservative strategy can pay off.

BETTING (OPTIONAL)
------------------
When betting is enabled, you place chips before each hand.
