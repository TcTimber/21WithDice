21 With Dice

Beat the dealer without going over 21

About the Game

21 With Dice is a fast-paced dice game where you roll against the dealer (computer) to get as close to 21 as possible. Choose how many dice to roll each turn, manage your risk, and beat the dealer's total to win.

  • Play against a dealer that follows fixed house rules
  • Roll 1, 2, or 3 dice — up to 4 times per hand
  • Three sixes on your first roll wins instantly
  • Local top-10 leaderboard tracks your wins
  • iPhone and iPad support

How to Play

The dealer rolls first, revealing only one die. Then it's your turn:

  • Choose to roll 1, 2, or 3 dice. Their values add to your running total.
  • After each roll, tap Stand to keep your total, or roll again.
  • You may roll up to 4 times per hand.
  • Go over 21 and you bust — the dealer wins automatically.
  • After you stand, the dealer reveals its hidden dice and completes its hand.
  • The dealer must roll again if at 16 or below, and must stand at 17 or higher.
  • Highest total at or below 21 wins. Ties go to the dealer.

Instant Win: Roll three sixes (18) on your very first roll and you win immediately — the dealer doesn't get to finish.

Odds Table

Once your running total reaches 10, an odds table appears on the left side of the play area to help you decide how many dice to roll next. Each row corresponds to a roll option (1, 2, or 3 dice), and each column shows a probability:

  • 🍀 21 — the chance that this roll lands you exactly on 21 (an instant standing total).
  • 17+ — the chance that the roll puts you in the 17–21 range, a strong standing total.
  • 💀 — the chance that the roll pushes you over 21 and busts your hand.

All percentages are computed from the exact probability of every dice combination — no estimation. Values are shown to one decimal place. A few special cases:

  • N/A means the outcome is impossible (0%). For example, you can't bust with one die if you're at 14 (max roll is 6, total 20).
  • A red X means the outcome is certain (100%). For example, with a total of 18, rolling 3 dice will always reach 17 or better — but it will also always exceed 18, often pushing you to bust.

Use the table to weigh aggressive vs. safe plays. The table hides during the dealer's turn and between hands.

Leaderboard

Optionally enter a player name on first launch to track your win total on a local top-10 leaderboard. The leaderboard is stored only on your device and is never uploaded or shared. You can change your name or reset the leaderboard at any time from the Settings menu.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the dealer win ties?

Just like blackjack, ties go to the house. It's part of the house edge and keeps the game balanced.

Can the dealer bust?

Yes — the dealer must keep rolling when at 16 or below, which means the dealer can go over 21. A conservative strategy of standing early can pay off.

What does the dealer's revealed die tell me?

It's a clue to the dealer's strength. A high visible die suggests the dealer may already have a strong hand. A low die could mean opportunity — or a bluff.

Is rolling more dice always better?

Not necessarily. Rolling 3 dice at once grows your total quickly but increases bust risk. Rolling 1 die is safer but slower. Mix it up based on your current total and how far you are from 21.

What iOS version is required?

The app requires iOS 17 or later. It runs on both iPhone and iPad.
